Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Snuggle Pets does not collect personal information from anyone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no network requests made by the app.

What stays on your device

Your child's pets (their names, looks, outfits and houses), how they are doing, and the settings (bedtime, reminder, sounds, vibration) are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Pet names

The app suggests made-up names. A grown-up can type a different one. The name stays on the device and is only shown inside the app and in the optional reminder.

The daily reminder

The reminder is off until a grown-up turns it on in the grown-ups section. It is a local notification created on the device, at most once a day. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Children's privacy

Snuggle Pets is made for children aged 4 to 8 and is offered in the Kids Category of the App Store. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children, so there is nothing for us to share, sell or use for advertising.

  • The app never asks for a photo, voice recording or location, and never uses the camera or microphone.
  • No third party receives any information from the app. It contains no ads, analytics or other third-party code.
  • Purchases, settings and all links to outside websites are in the grown-ups section behind a parental gate.
  • To delete a pet, use the Pets list in the grown-ups section, or delete the app.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase made through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details or Apple Account.

If you write to us

If you email us, we see your email address and message and use them only to answer you.

Changes and contact
